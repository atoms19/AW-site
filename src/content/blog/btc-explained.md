# how to pay 

so like i got some btc like 4btc from this transaction Tx0 and i wanna send it to my friend bob
first i create a new transaction Tx1 with an message 
"yo im sending bob some money after this transaction i will have 2btc and bob will have 2btc"

in reality its like i include bobs public key in the transaction ( so that anyone can identify its him ) along with the input which includes the split , what i have remaining and what he will get 
and then i sign it with my private key to prove that its me sending the money  also i use the hash of tx0 to show that i am using the money i got from tx0 to send to bob 

how does people know this transaction is legit ? , they can use my public key to decrypt the transaction and verify its me who sent money , the person i send money to 
and they can follow up the chain like the previous transactions to verify that i actually have the money to send and that i didnt double spend it
