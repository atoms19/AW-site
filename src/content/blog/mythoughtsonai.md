# cost of automation

webdevs are not the most easily replaceable profession , there are tons of other profession that could be automated by AI 
but webdevs are the ones in the most demand for being replaced 
companies are looking for ways to cut costs and increase efficiency
and webdevs are costly and time consuming to hire and train
so they turn to ai 


ai itself will not replace webdevs, as it makes mistake its like a stupid intern who knows a lot of things 
but does not know where exactly things go , but thats not the point
suppose you are a person who knows how to manage these interns give them small tasks induvidually to complete and then u yourself tell anotehr slightly smarter intern to put things together 
bit by bit or do that yourself , you went from a single dev writing components.tsx file to someone who has an entire team in his hands
all you have to do is review the code and make sure it works 

it works i have tried , backwards mentality of us is whats preventing us from accepting LLMs code and working on top of it 
most experineced webdevs sees it as jargon but think of it even if u were to write this jargon how long would it take you 
the point is not to use a single llm but multiple llms instances to do diffrent tasks that do not interfere with each other and put them together

sometimes you need context from one llm to another this is where docs comes in 
docs are a great way to document the code for llm s to understand the context 
suddenly you are a project manager with a team of interns

no longer the senior dev who writes code
you are the one who manages the interns but you yourself could be an intern
